Privacy Policy for Econz Signature Pro

Last Updated: July 2026

1. Overview

Econz Signature Pro ("the Application") is a Google Workspace Add-on developed by Econz to streamline and automate Gmail signature deployments across Google Workspace organizations. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting all data processed through our application.

2. Data Access and Scope

The Application requests access strictly to the minimum necessary Google API scopes to function:

  • Google Sheets Data: Read-only access to active spreadsheet content to pull user details (e.g., names, titles, contact information) for signature generation.
  • Gmail API: Authorized modification access (`gmail.settings.basic`, `gmail.settings.sharing`) used solely to push updated signature HTML layouts to specified Gmail accounts via service account delegation.

3. Data Storage and Retention

The Application operates entirely within your Google Workspace environment:

  • No External Storage: We do not store, log, or transmit any user profile data, spreadsheet content, or service account credentials to external Econz servers.
  • Local Script Properties: Configuration keys and template designs remain strictly inside your Google Spreadsheet's protected `PropertiesService` storage.

4. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We do not share, sell, rent, or trade any personal data or organizational information with third parties. All requests are processed directly between your Google Workspace environment and Google’s official APIs.

5. Google API Limited Use Compliance

Econz Signature Pro's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
